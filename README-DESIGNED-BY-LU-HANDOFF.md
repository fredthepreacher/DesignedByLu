# Designed By Lu — Website Handoff Notes

This package is the refined static foundation for Designed By Lu.

## Brand direction
Designed By Lu is the umbrella lifestyle collective. The five pillar gateways are:

1. Celebrate Always — weddings, events, and milestone celebrations.
2. If The Ring Could Talk — podcast and conversations around love, faith, identity, healing, and growth.
3. Reimagined & Restored — home furnishings, interiors, restored pieces, and room renewal.
4. Let’s Face It — beauty, confidence, faith, wellness, and personal renewal.
5. Off We Go — travel, lifestyle experiences, exploration, and renewal.

## What was refined
- Gateway cards now use cleaner high-resolution photographic treatments instead of crowded full-PDF screenshots.
- Homepage and pillar-page copy was rewritten to align with the PDFs: faith, restoration, celebration, confidence, travel, and purposeful living.
- Netlify-compatible contact form and thank-you page remain intact.
