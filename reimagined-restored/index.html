The experience

Good bones deserve another chapter.

Reimagined & Restored is the home edition of Designed By Lu. It honors timeless craftsmanship, thoughtful restoration, and pieces designed to be loved again. Rather than discard what still has story, this pillar reimagines rooms and furnishings with beauty, character, and purpose.

What guides the work

What guides this expression.

01

Honor what remains

Restoration begins by seeing the value that is still present beneath age, wear, or neglect.

02

Elevate without erasing

The goal is to refine the piece while respecting the story, structure, and craftsmanship it already carries.

03

Beauty should be lived in

A restored home should feel elegant, useful, personal, and ready for real life.
